Frequently Asked Questions

What is DramaStream?

DramaStream (fulldrama.stream) is a free streaming site for short dramas and vertical series. Every title plays in full — all episodes back to back, in your browser, with no sign-up and no fees. The catalog has over 25,000 series across romance, billionaire, revenge, werewolf and more.

Is it really free to watch short dramas here?

Yes. Every episode of every drama streams free — no subscription, no paywall, no hidden fees, and no episode limits.

Do I need an app or an account?

No. DramaStream runs entirely in your browser on phones, tablets, laptops and desktops. There is no app to download and no account to create — open the site and start watching.

What are short dramas (vertical series)?

Short dramas are bite-sized series made for phones: vertical video, episodes of one to three minutes, and fast-paced stories you can binge in one sitting. Popular genres include billionaire and CEO romances, revenge plots, werewolf and fated-mate fantasies, and hidden-identity thrillers.

What can I watch on DramaStream?

Over 25,000 English short dramas from platforms like DramaBox and FlickReels, spanning romance, billionaire & CEO, revenge, werewolf, contract marriage, divorce & exes and more. Use the Popular and Ratings tabs to see the highest-rated titles, or New for the latest additions.

How often are new dramas added?

New releases are added daily. Tap the New tab (or open fulldrama.stream/?sort=new) to see everything added most recently first.

How does DramaStream handle copyright?

DramaStream does not host video files — streams are embedded from third-party sources. If you are a rights holder and believe content infringes your copyright, contact us through the DMCA page and we will process your request.
